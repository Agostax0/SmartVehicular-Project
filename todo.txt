A depth camera is identical to your RGB camera but returns an image where the color of each pixel represents the exact distance (depth) in meters from the lens.

How it works:
You spawn a Depth Camera at the exact same location as the RGB camera.
When YOLO detects the walker's bounding box on the RGB image, you look at the same pixel coordinates (cx, cy) on the Depth Camera's output frame.
The value at that pixel gives you the exact depth $Z$ in meters!
You calculate the lateral distance $X$ in meters using simple camera trigonometry (camera field of view): $$X = Z \times \tan\left(\text{horizontal angle offset from center}\right)$$
Best for: A highly realistic, industry-standard camera setup. It is very fast and doesn't require heavy calculations.
